# Appalachian autumn wedding design

Built from the published `main` branch, preserving Jeremiah and Nichole's portrait, wedding details, map destination and calendar event.

## Artwork
Generated using the built-in image generation tool; optimized WebP files are used on the page. The landscape JPEG is provided for social sharing.

- `assets/appalachian-autumn-v2.webp` / `assets/appalachian-autumn-social-v2.jpg`: wide photographic Appalachian golden-hour landscape, autumn oak and maple trees, wedding string lights, sunflower corners, and quiet space for editable HTML text. No people, text or logos. Evocative scenery, not an image of the actual venue.
- `assets/sunflower-garland-v2.webp`: transparent botanical watercolor garland with three gold sunflowers, copper maple and oak leaves, olive foliage, and small cream wildflowers; no text, vase, frame or ground.

## Behavior
- Responsive layout, anchor navigation, clear directions and calendar actions.
- Honors reduced motion, supports keyboard focus and keeps information available without JavaScript.
- Countdown uses the original September 19, 2026, 3:00 pm Eastern time and switches to thanks at midnight after the wedding.
